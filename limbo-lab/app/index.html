Limbo scored no higher than pure noise. Its biggest apparent "pattern" (|z| 2.67) was actually smaller than the one the random control threw up (|z| 3.28) — both are ordinary luck across 124 tests. When the most extreme results from one seed pair were re-checked on five others, none repeated.

To prove the tests can find a pattern, a fake one was planted — a win made 2 points likelier after a loss. A neural network caught it instantly (z ≈ 16). Run on the real results, the same network scored z ≈ 1.2: nothing to learn.

This is what "provably fair" buys you: not a way to win, but a way to check you weren't cheated. Use the Verify tab for exactly that.

The edge is mathematical, not beatable. No seed, streak, system, or model changes the 1% the house keeps on average. This tool is for verification and understanding the odds — not a betting strategy. If gambling stops being fun, step away.
